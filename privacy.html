Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is a photo gallery run by Joyal. It has no accounts, forms or ads. It uses Google Analytics to count visits. You can turn this off at any time on this page.

Who is responsible

This website (by.joyal, at joyal.dev) is run by Joyal. You can contact me by message on Instagram or Facebook about anything in this policy.

What I collect

I do not ask you for any personal information, and the site has no forms, comments or sign-ups. The only data collected is usage data through Google Analytics, which does not include your name or contact details.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4), a service provided by Google, to understand how many people visit and which devices and countries they come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers stored on your device. It collects information such as:

  • the pages you view and when
  • how you arrived (for example, from a link or a search)
  • your approximate location, such as country or city
  • your device, browser and screen size

Google processes this data on my behalf and may process it in the United States and other countries. I use it only to see how the site is used. I do not combine it with any other information about you, and I do not use it for advertising.

To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets these cookies on your device when you visit:

  • _ga tells visitors apart. It lasts up to two years.
  • _ga_<ID> keeps track of your current visit. It also lasts up to two years.

If you turn analytics off on this page, this site saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it is remembered. It contains only the word "denied".

Your choices

You can turn Google Analytics off for this site at any time, and back on again if you change your mind:

Turning analytics off stops any further collection and also deletes the Google Analytics cookies this site set on your device. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, to ask for it to be deleted, and to object to or stop the use of your data. Because the analytics data does not include your name or contact details and is held by Google, I generally cannot look up an individual visitor. If you have a question or complaint, contact me using the links above. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
